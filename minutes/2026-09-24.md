## cashu-ts

**[v5.0.0-rc.8](https://github.com/cashubtc/cashu-ts/releases/tag/v5.0.0-rc.8) · [rc.10](https://github.com/cashubtc/cashu-ts/releases/tag/v5.0.0-rc.10) · [rc.11](https://github.com/cashubtc/cashu-ts/releases/tag/v5.0.0-rc.11)**
- Nutroot secrets for v3 (BLS) keysets; P2PK APIs renamed to Lock (`LockBuilder`, `asLocked`).
- Broad hardening pass, AbortSignal support, crash-safe swap previews and `findCashuPayload`. Breaking changes are listed in migration-5.0.0.md.

**[v4.10.0](https://github.com/cashubtc/cashu-ts/releases/tag/v4.10.0) · [4.10.1](https://github.com/cashubtc/cashu-ts/releases/tag/v4.10.1) · [4.10.2](https://github.com/cashubtc/cashu-ts/releases/tag/v4.10.2) · [4.11.0](https://github.com/cashubtc/cashu-ts/releases/tag/v4.11.0)**
